Our Story

A Legacy of Royal Vegetarian Cuisine

Nestled in the heart of Gopiganj, Bhadohi, Rajpoot Dhaba has been a beloved landmark for food lovers across Uttar Pradesh. Founded with one simple promise — to serve honest, delicious, 100% pure vegetarian food — we've grown into a destination that families, travellers, and locals return to again and again.

Our kitchen is a sacred space where traditional recipes meet the finest seasonal ingredients. Every dish is prepared fresh, every single day — never compromising on purity, taste, or love.
